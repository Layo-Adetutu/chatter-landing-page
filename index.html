Our story

About Chatter

Chatter is a multi-functional platform where authors and readers can have access to their own content. It aims to be a traditional bookworm's heaven and a blog to get access to more text-based content.

Our vision is to foster an inclusive and vibrant community where diversity is celebrated. We encourage open-mindedness and respect for all individuals, regardless of their backgrounds or beliefs. By promoting dialogue and understanding, we strive to create a space where every voice matters.

Why you should join Chatter

Our goal is to make writers and readers see our platform as their next haven for blogging, ensuring ease in interactions, connecting with like-minded peers, accessing favourite content based on interests and sharing great ideas with people.

Analytics

Analytics to track the number of views, likes and comments, and also analyse the performance of your articles over a period of time.

Social interactions

Users on the platform can interact with posts they like, comment and engage in discussions.

Content creation

Write nice and appealing content with our in-built markdown, a rich text editor designed to make sharing your ideas simple.
